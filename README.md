# 🔔 Remyndr – College Circular Management & Student Productivity Web App

**Every Notice. On Time.**

🌐 **Live Project**  
 https://bhoomika-ganesh.github.io/Remyndr/

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Remyndr is a modern web application designed to help college students manage circulars, academic announcements, tasks, deadlines, fee payments, and important events in one centralized platform.

The platform aims to reduce missed announcements, simplify deadline management, and make college life more organized through an intuitive, interactive, and user-friendly interface.

---

## 🚀 Features

📊 **Smart Dashboard** – Overview of recent notices, upcoming deadlines, pending tasks, and important activities.

📢 **College Circular Management** – Centralized access to college circulars, document uploads, and important notice information.

🔄 **Circular Updates** – Review revised circulars, compare changes, and confirm updates before publishing.

📅 **Academic Calendar** – Monthly calendar to view academic events, important dates, and personal reminders.

✅ **Smart Checklists & Tasks** – Organize academic responsibilities and track pending and completed tasks.

💰 **Fee Tracker** – View fee records, monitor payment deadlines, and track paid and pending statuses.

🤖 **Ask Remyndr AI** – Assistant interface for finding relevant information within the application, depending on the configured AI functionality.

🔔 **Notifications & Reminders** – Keep track of important updates, college activities, and deadlines.

🌙 **Light & Dark Mode** – Switch between themes for a comfortable viewing experience.

🌐 **Multilingual Interface** – Language selection for English, Hindi, and Kannada.

👤 **Login & Account Creation** – User interface for account access and profile management.

🎨 **Modern UI Design** – Attractive visuals, interactive elements, and a student-friendly experience.

📱 **Responsive Layout** – Designed for convenient use across supported screen sizes.

## 🎯 Purpose of the Project

Remyndr was developed as a student innovation project to address the challenges college students face when managing announcements, academic responsibilities, deadlines, and fee payments.

The goal is to bring essential college information and productivity tools together in one platform, helping students stay informed, organized, and on time.
